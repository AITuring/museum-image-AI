## Design Context

### Users
- Primary user is an individual collector/curator organizing personal museum and artifact photos.
- Core jobs are uploading, identifying, editing, searching, and browsing artifact records with low friction.
- The interface should support both focused data entry and calm long-form browsing of collected material.

### Brand Personality
- 3-word personality: refined, quiet, editorial.
- The product should feel trustworthy, cultured, and composed rather than startup-like or overly technical.
- Emotional goal: make collecting and reviewing artifacts feel intentional, curated, and satisfying.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Use an editorial exhibition aesthetic inspired by museum catalogues and reading-note layouts.
- Favor warm neutrals, restrained contrast, serif-led hierarchy, compact spacing, and dense but breathable composition.
- Avoid purple-blue SaaS gradients, oversized soft cards, flashy decoration, and noisy motion.
- Default direction is light mode.

### Design Principles
- Make information feel curated, not dashboard-like.
- Use space efficiently with tighter rhythm and clearer hierarchy.
- Prefer section-based layout over repeated floating cards.
- Let typography and tone carry the visual identity before decoration.
- Keep interactions understated, fast, and purposeful.
